Sales performance · Q1 2025

Regional revenue analysis

Analyzed transaction-level sales data to calculate revenue for a specific city and month. The calculation filters to Mumbai transactions in February 2025, computes each row's revenue as quantity multiplied by unit price, then sums the matching rows.

Region
Mumbai
City filter
Reporting period
Feb 2025
Month filter
Metric
Revenue
SUM(quantity × price)

Method

  1. Load sales_data.csv and parse the transaction date.
  2. Filter rows where city is Mumbai and the date falls in February 2025.
  3. Calculate transaction revenue as quantity × price.
  4. Sum revenue for all matching transactions and round to the nearest integer.

Quality check: The city and month filters must both match. Checking the year as well prevents transactions from other February periods from being included.

Approach

Data preparation

Inspect column types and normalize date fields before filtering.

Metric calculation

Use a row-level revenue calculation, then aggregate only the selected transactions.

Clear communication

State the reporting period, dimensions, formula, and rounding convention alongside each result.
